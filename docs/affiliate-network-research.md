# Affiliate Network Research — Which to Apply to First

Goal: identify which flight/hotel/card affiliate programs a solo/early-stage builder can realistically get approved for *before* Nestflight has meaningful traffic, so "Book now" is a real revenue event in Phase 1–2, not a Phase 3 aspiration.

## Bottom line

Apply in this order: **TravelPayouts first** (get something live this week), **Awin second** (for Booking.com access), **FlexOffers or CardRatings third** (for the credit-card revenue line). Skip Skyscanner's direct program and CJ Affiliate until there's real traffic to point to.

---

## Flights & hotels

| Network | Fit for a new/small site | Requirements | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TravelPayouts** | **Best starting point** | No minimum traffic requirement; approval can be near-instant for many of the 100+ bundled brands (Booking.com, Expedia, GetYourGuide, car rental, and more) | One dashboard, one payout wallet, instead of juggling separate applications per brand. This is the fastest path to a working "Book now" link while Nestflight has near-zero traffic. |
| **Awin** (hosts Booking.com for US/APAC/LatAm affiliates) | Accessible | Basic publisher account verification + a description of traffic sources; lower bar than going direct | Booking.com pays ~4% of booking value on accommodations, scaling with volume; this is the more durable long-term hotel relationship once there's real traffic. |
| **CJ Affiliate** (hosts Booking.com for EU/UK/Middle East affiliates) | Harder for a new site | Same idea as Awin but reportedly a higher acceptance bar and slower payouts for newer publishers | Lower priority unless the audience is EU-based. |
| **Skyscanner direct (via Impact)** | Hardest for a pre-launch site | Fully functional HTTPS site, 10–15 published on-topic posts, working About + privacy policy, real traffic or a proven social audience; does not accept coupon/subnetwork/tech-partner models | Apply once Nestflight has content and traffic to show — not at Phase 0/1. Commission is modest (industry-typical ~5% on flights) so it's a volume play, not worth chasing early. |

## Credit cards (the highest-payout-per-conversion line)

| Network | Fit for a new/small site | Requirements | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CardRatings** | **Best starting point** | Sub-affiliate model — you promote CardRatings' existing content/relationships rather than applying to each card issuer directly | Good for a pre-launch or low-traffic site: borrows someone else's compliance and disclosure infrastructure. Lower per-approval payout than going direct, but zero cold-start problem. |
| **FlexOffers** | Accessible | General affiliate network account; reported to be more beginner-friendly than CJ/Impact for credit card offers specifically | Broad card issuer selection; worth applying once there's a working site with a privacy policy and disclosures in place. |
| **Impact / CJ Affiliate (direct card issuer programs)** | Harder for a new site | Higher acceptance bar, slower payouts, typically expects an established site with traffic | Revisit in Phase 2–3 once Nestflight has real visitors; commissions here run $50–200+ per approved card, the single highest-value conversion type in the whole revenue mix. |

---

## What this means for the roadmap

- **This week (Phase 0/1):** Apply to TravelPayouts. It has no minimum-traffic gate, so the "Book now" buttons in the prototype can point to a real, revenue-generating link almost immediately, even before the waitlist has real volume.
- **Once the site is live with a privacy policy and a few real pages (Phase 1):** Apply to Awin for durable Booking.com access, and to CardRatings for the card-referral line.
- **Once there's real traffic to show (Phase 2):** Apply directly to Skyscanner (via Impact) and consider FlexOffers or direct card-issuer programs for better per-conversion economics than the sub-affiliate arrangements.
- **Compliance note:** every network above requires a visible privacy policy and (for the card programs especially) clear affiliate-disclosure language — both are cheap to do now and worth building into the site before the first application, not after a rejection.
