## How it works (3 steps)

**1. Build your family's travel profile**
A couple of taps each for everyone in the family — travel style, interests, and for the kids, their school. Takes about 90 seconds.

**2. We find your windows**
We overlap every kid's school calendar to find the trips big enough to matter — and the sneaky long weekends most families miss entirely.

**3. Get matched, not just searched**
Destinations scored against the season, your family's shared interests, and what's actually on sale right now — including deals only points-and-miles people usually catch.

## FAQ

**Is this only for US schools?**
We're starting with US public school calendars. If your kids are homeschooled or on a flexible schedule, you can set that too.

**Do I need a lot of points and miles to use this?**
No — the calendar and destination matching work with zero points. The points tools are there if you have them.

**When do I get access?**
We're inviting people in small batches, starting with families who join the waitlist and answer a couple of quick questions about how they currently plan trips.
