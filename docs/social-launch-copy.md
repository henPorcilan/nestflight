## Reddit — general notes before posting

Check each subreddit's self-promotion rules first — several require a minimum account history/karma before linking your own project, and some ban it outright. Where allowed, frame it as "I'm building X, want feedback" rather than a pitch — that's what these communities respond to.

**r/Mommit or r/daddit** (parenting-focused, generally receptive to "building something for parents, want your take" posts):

> Title: Building a tool to plan family trips around school calendars — would love parent feedback
>
> Body: I keep running into the same problem: figuring out which of my kids' school breaks line up with a decent flight/hotel window before prices spike. Started building something to automate that (school calendar matching + destination/season scoring + deal tracking), but before I build more I want to know if this is actually a real pain point for other families or just mine.
>
> If you've got 5 minutes: [survey link]. Early concept page here if curious: https://henporcilan.github.io/nestflight/. Not selling anything — just trying to validate before I sink more time in.

**r/travel or r/travelhacks** (more deal/points-focused audience):

> Title: Building a tool that matches school breaks to flight deals and points sweet spots — feedback wanted
>
> Body: Frequent-flyer instincts meet parent logistics: I'm building a tool that cross-references kids' school calendars with ideal travel timing, then surfaces flight/hotel deals and points redemptions for that window. Concept/landing page: https://henporcilan.github.io/nestflight/. If you're a parent who travels with points/miles, I'd love feedback: [survey link].
