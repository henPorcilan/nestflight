Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Nestflight is a family trip-planning concept currently in a pre-launch validation phase. This page explains what information is collected through this site and the interactive prototype, and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Waitlist signups: if you join the waitlist, we collect the email address (and any optional answers) you submit through the sign-up form.
  • Prototype usage: the interactive prototype at /prototype/ runs entirely in your browser. Any family profile, school, or trip information you enter into the prototype stays in your browser's memory for that session and is not transmitted to or stored on any Nestflight server.
  • Basic site analytics: standard, aggregate technical data (such as page views) may be collected by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) in the normal course of serving this site.

How we use it

  • Waitlist emails are used only to notify you about Nestflight access and occasional updates related to the product. We do not sell or share your email with third parties.
  • We do not use waitlist or prototype data for advertising, and we do not share personal data with data brokers.

Children's information

Nestflight is intended for use by parents and guardians planning family trips. Any information about children (such as a school name or age band) entered into the prototype is provided by the parent, stays in-browser only, and is never transmitted or stored by Nestflight in this pre-launch phase.

Third-party links

This site may link to third-party services, including a waitlist form (Google Forms) and, once integrated, affiliate travel-booking partners. Those services have their own privacy policies governing the information you provide directly to them.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from waitlist emails at any time by replying to any email you receive from us. To request removal of your waitlist email before then, contact us via the project's GitHub page.

Changes to this policy

As Nestflight moves from validation into a live product, this policy will be updated to reflect any new data practices (for example, account creation or booking integrations). We'll update the "last updated" date above when that happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the GitHub Issues page for this project.
